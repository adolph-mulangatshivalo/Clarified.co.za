By The Clarified Editorial Team.

Engineering is one of those careers where the money can get pretty good once you have the right qualification, experience and skills.

But not all engineering jobs pay the same.

So, if you're thinking about studying engineering and wondering which engineering jobs pay the most in South Africa, here's a look at some of the highest paying options.

Note: These are estimated monthly salary ranges, not guaranteed salaries. What you earn depends on your experience, employer, industry, location, qualifications and professional registration. Senior and specialist engineers can earn well above the normal range, while graduates will usually start much lower. Salary sources also use different methods, so there isn't one official salary figure for every engineering job.

1. Mining Engineer

Salary: R30,000 – R130,000+ per month

Mining engineers plan and manage mining operations.

They work out how minerals can be extracted safely and efficiently, while dealing with things like mine design, equipment, production and safety.

South Africa has a massive mining industry, so there is good demand for people with the right skills.

The salary can increase a lot as you gain experience and move into senior or specialist positions.

2. Petroleum Engineer

Salary: R35,000 – R150,000+ per month

Petroleum engineers work with oil and gas.

They help find, extract and produce oil and natural gas, as well as designing ways to make the extraction process more efficient.

It's a very specialised field, which is one reason experienced petroleum engineers can earn a lot.

The downside is that the South African market is much smaller than fields like mining, civil or electrical engineering.

3. Chemical Engineer

Salary: R30,000 – R120,000+ per month

Chemical engineers work out how to turn raw materials into useful products on a large scale.

They can work in industries such as mining, chemicals, manufacturing, energy, food and pharmaceuticals.

It's not just about mixing chemicals in a lab. A lot of the work involves designing and improving industrial processes.

4. Electrical Engineer

Salary: R30,000 – R120,000+ per month

Electrical engineers work with electricity, power systems, electrical equipment and infrastructure.

In South Africa, they can work in mining, manufacturing, construction, energy, telecommunications and renewable energy.

It's also a pretty flexible engineering career because electrical systems are needed across a lot of industries.

5. Process Engineer

Salary: R30,000 – R120,000+ per month

Process engineers focus on how things are made.

They look for ways to make industrial processes safer, cheaper and more efficient.

You can find process engineers working in mining, manufacturing, chemicals, energy, food production and other industries.

Basically, if a company has a complicated process that needs to run properly, a process engineer can be involved.

6. Metallurgical Engineer

Salary: R30,000 – R135,000+ per month

Metallurgical engineers work with metals and materials.

They look at how metals are extracted, processed and improved so they can be used in different products.

This is particularly useful in South Africa because of the country's mining and mineral-processing industries.

It's also a fairly specialised career, so experienced people with the right skills can earn very well.
