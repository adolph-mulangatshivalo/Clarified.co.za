Mamelodi Sundowns vs Marumo Gallants: 2026 Kick-off Time and Preview

Mamelodi Sundowns return to Loftus Versfeld for a crucial Betway Premiership clash against Marumo Gallants. Here’s everything you need to know about the kick-off time, venue, broadcast, ticket prices, form and what to expect.

WHAT TO EXPECT

  • Mamelodi Sundowns vs Marumo Gallants: Current form
  • What the coach and players are saying
  • What we don't know yet
  • Sources

The clash we’ve been waiting for, Mamelodi Sundowns vs Marumo Gallants will take place on Wednesday, 19 August 2026, at Loftus Versfeld in Pretoria. Kick-off is at 19:30 SAST. The match will be shown live on SuperSport.

This is Sundowns' first home league game of the season. It's a catch-up fixture — the original season opener was postponed after the death of Sundowns midfielder Jayden Adams disrupted the club's pre-season plans. The Premier Soccer League (PSL) approved the new date.

VenueLoftus Versfeld, Pretoria
BroadcastSuperSport
Ticket priceR50

Mamelodi Sundowns vs Marumo Gallants: Current form

Sundowns sit 7th on the table with 4 points from 2 games (1 win, 1 draw). They beat TS Galaxy 3-2 and drew 1-1 with Kaizer Chiefs, with Mailula scoring the equaliser.

Gallants are 14th with 1 point from 2 games (0 wins, 1 draw, 1 loss). They drew 0-0 with Kruger United, then lost 3-1 to Stellenbosch.

Position Points Record (W-D-L) Goals (For-Against)
Sundowns 7th 4 1-1-0 4-3
Gallants 14th 1 0-1-1 1-3

Sundowns have never lost to Gallants in the league. Since Gallants' promotion in 2021, Sundowns have won 6 and drawn 2 of their 8 league meetings. Gallants' only wins over Sundowns have come on penalties in cup games, including last season's Nedbank Cup quarter-final.

What the coach and players are saying

Sundowns coach Miguel Cardoso expects Gallants to be aggressive in transition, using their pace to attack spaces.

Defender Grant Kekana said the club wants to make a strong statement in front of its home fans, and that this season's new signings have the quality to help Sundowns win more trophies.

What we don't know yet

Team line-ups and any injury or availability news have not been confirmed. This article will be updated closer to kick-off if that changes.
